GUIDE · LONG CHAT CONTINUITY

When ChatGPT loses the thread, preserve the project — not the entire transcript.

A long AI conversation can become awkward to continue, and a fresh chat starts without the working state you built up. The useful fix is not blindly copying thousands of messages. It is carrying forward the parts that still govern the work.

What usually disappears when you start fresh

The costly loss is project state: the exact goal, choices already rejected, decisions already made, constraints, important reported results, unresolved blockers, and the next action. Those facts are scattered through the conversation, so rebuilding them manually gets slower as the project grows.

Make a handoff that a new chat can actually use

A good handoff should be short enough to review and structured enough to distinguish a decision from a plan. Keep recent messages only when their wording or evidence still matters. Keep durable state separately so a fresh conversation can understand where the project stands without replaying every historical turn.

Do not confuse project continuity with provider memory

Personalisation or saved preferences can be useful, but they are not the same as the current state of a specific project. A project can change daily. It may also move to another AI provider. Keeping its state in a portable, user-reviewed handoff makes that continuity independent of one conversation.

A simple recovery checklist

  • Write the current objective in one or two sentences.
  • List decisions that should not be reopened without new evidence.
  • Keep constraints and non-negotiable rules explicit.
  • Separate completed or reported results from planned work.
  • End with blockers and the next concrete actions.
