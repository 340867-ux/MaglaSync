GUIDE · CROSS-AI CONTINUITY

Move the work between ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini — not just a wall of transcript.

Switching AI tools is easy. Rebuilding the project context is the expensive part. A useful transfer gives the next chat enough state to continue immediately while keeping the handoff small enough for you to inspect.

There are two basic transfer strategies

Transcript transfer carries much of the conversation forward. It can be useful when the exact wording and dialogue history matter. Project-state transfer carries the current objective, decisions, constraints, important results, blockers, and next steps. That is often better for work that has already changed direction several times.

What the receiving AI actually needs

  • The current goal and what success means.
  • Decisions that should remain settled.
  • Constraints, rules, and assumptions still in force.
  • Important results, with unverified AI claims clearly distinguished.
  • Open blockers and the immediate next actions.
  • Recent messages only when their exact detail still matters.
MaglaSync's approach

MaglaSync Free keeps one project journal locally in the browser. Every AI chat starts disconnected; you connect only the conversations that belong to the project. Before context is placed in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, you can review and edit the handoff. You press Send yourself.

Why this is different from importing chat history

MaglaSync does not claim to recreate one provider's native history inside another provider. It prepares a portable working brief for the next conversation. That makes the same project usable across tools without pretending the destination platform owns the original conversation.
