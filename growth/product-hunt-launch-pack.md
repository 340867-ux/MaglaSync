# MaglaSync Product Hunt Launch Pack

## Positioning
MaglaSync Free is a free, open-source, local-first browser extension that carries project context between ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini without a MaglaSync account, cloud backend, analytics, or freemium limit.

## Tagline options
1. Local project memory across ChatGPT, Claude & Gemini
2. Stop re-explaining your project to every AI
3. Free open-source continuity across your AI chats

## Short description
Keep a lightweight project journal locally and reuse reviewed context across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. No account, no AI API key, no cloud backend, and no paid tier.

## Maker first comment
I built MaglaSync because switching between ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini kept resetting the project context. The goal is deliberately narrower than “store every chat forever”: preserve the project state you actually want to carry forward, keep it local, review it, and hand it to the next AI when you choose.

MaglaSync Free is open source and completely free. It does not require a MaglaSync account or AI API key, does not send your project journal to a MaglaSync server, and ships for both Chrome and Firefox.

What I would value most from Product Hunt users is feedback on three things: whether the handoff feels faster than copy/paste, whether the journal captures the right amount of context, and where the browser workflow still feels awkward.

## FAQ
### Does it upload my chats to MaglaSync?
The project journal is stored locally by the extension. MaglaSync Free does not require a MaglaSync cloud account or backend to work.

### Is it a full chat migration tool?
No. It focuses on reusable project continuity rather than blindly copying an entire transcript.

### Is there a paid tier?
No. MaglaSync Free is free and open source.

### Which browsers and AI tools?
Current public targets are Chrome and Firefox, with project continuity workflows for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.

### Does it send prompts automatically?
No. The user reviews the handoff and remains in control of sending.
